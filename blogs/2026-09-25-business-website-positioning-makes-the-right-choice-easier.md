A visitor should not need a sales call to understand who a business is best equipped to help. Business website positioning makes that fit visible through the audience named, the problem framed, the outcome promised, and the evidence selected. It helps the right visitor recognize a relevant choice while giving everyone else an honest basis for deciding.
