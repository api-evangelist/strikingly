Personalization should make a website easier to use, not make visitors wonder how much the business knows about them. Website content personalization works best when it responds to clear signals: the page someone opened, a category they chose, a topic they subscribed to, a language preference, or an answer they deliberately provided. The goal is not a different website for every person.
