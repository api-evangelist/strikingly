A visitor does not become a useful lead simply because an email address reaches a database. A strong lead generation website creates a fair exchange: the visitor receives clarity, help, or access, while the business receives enough context to continue a relevant conversation. That exchange should feel timely and proportionate, never like a toll placed in front of basic information.
