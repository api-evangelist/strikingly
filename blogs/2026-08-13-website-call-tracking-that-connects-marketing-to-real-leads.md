A phone call can be one of a website's most valuable conversions and one of its easiest outcomes to misattribute. The caller may have arrived through an advertisement, local search result, referral, email, or direct visit. Without website call tracking, the team hears the conversation but cannot reliably connect it to the marketing path that created it.
