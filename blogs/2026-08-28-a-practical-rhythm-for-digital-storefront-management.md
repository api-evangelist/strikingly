An online store changes even when its design stays the same. Products arrive, stock moves, offers expire, and customers reveal new ways of shopping. Digital storefront management keeps the public store synchronized with those changes so a shopper does not encounter yesterday's assortment, an unavailable promise, or a promotion that no longer makes sense.
