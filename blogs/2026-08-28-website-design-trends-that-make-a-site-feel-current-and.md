A current-looking website is not a collage of whatever appeared in the latest inspiration feed. It is a system in which layout, type, imagery, spacing, controls, and responsive behavior support the same message. Website design trends are useful when they help visitors understand and act; they become noise when they are copied without regard for the site's audience.
