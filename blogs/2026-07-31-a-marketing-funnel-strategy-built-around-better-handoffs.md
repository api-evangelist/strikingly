A funnel rarely fails because a business forgot to draw three stages on a slide. It fails at the handoffs: an advertisement makes one promise while the landing page makes another, a helpful article ends without a useful next step, or a form submission triggers a generic message. A practical marketing funnel strategy connects those moments so moving visitors toward conversions feels like sensible continuation.
