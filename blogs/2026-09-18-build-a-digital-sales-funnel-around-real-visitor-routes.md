A digital sales funnel is easier to improve when it is drawn as a set of real visitor routes rather than a neat triangle. One person may arrive from a problem-focused search, another from a comparison ad, and a third from an email sent after an earlier visit. A digital sales funnel should not give them the same introduction, proof, or next action.
