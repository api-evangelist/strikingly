A rising visitor chart can look like progress while hiding an expensive problem. Online business growth is stronger when the website attracts relevant people from several dependable sources, helps them understand the offer, and turns enough of those visits into worthwhile customer relationships. Volume matters, but the path from attention to value matters more.
