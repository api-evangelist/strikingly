A brand does not earn a position by declaring that it is innovative, caring, or the best. People assign it after noticing a pattern: who the business helps, which problem it understands, what it does differently, and why that difference matters. Digital brand positioning makes that pattern recognizable across a website, search result, email, and social profile.
