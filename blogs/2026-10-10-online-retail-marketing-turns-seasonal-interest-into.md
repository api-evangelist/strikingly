A retail campaign is not finished when a banner goes live. Shoppers still need to recognize the offer, find suitable products, understand why those products fit the moment, and complete a purchase without losing the campaign context. Online retail marketing coordinates that entire route instead of treating advertisements, store pages, and reporting as separate projects.
