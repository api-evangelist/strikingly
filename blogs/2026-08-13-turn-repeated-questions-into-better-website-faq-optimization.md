An FAQ helps when it lets someone complete a task, understand a policy, or decide without assistance. Website FAQ optimization is the ongoing work of choosing real questions, writing usable answers, arranging them around visitor needs, and retiring unhelpful information. That makes an FAQ more than a long page of expandable text.
