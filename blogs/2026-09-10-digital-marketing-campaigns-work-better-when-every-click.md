An advertisement can promise one outcome while its landing page opens with a broader company message and the follow-up email introduces another offer. Digital marketing campaigns perform better when every handoff confirms that the visitor is still in the right place. Connecting promotional messages across channels gives the campaign a recognizable promise without forcing identical copy into every format.
