A satisfied quote can reassure a buyer, but customer success stories can do more: they show the situation, the work, and the change in enough detail for a reader to judge relevance. A strong story does not announce that a project was successful and move on. It explains what success meant to the client, how the result was observed, and where uncertainty still belongs.
