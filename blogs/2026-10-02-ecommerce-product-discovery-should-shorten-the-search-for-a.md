A shopper does not experience a catalog as a database. They experience decisions about where to begin, which products belong together, what should appear first, and whether a suggested item deserves attention. Ecommerce product discovery succeeds when those decisions feel understandable; reducing time spent searching follows as irrelevant choices disappear.
