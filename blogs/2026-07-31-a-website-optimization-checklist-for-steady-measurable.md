A useful website optimization checklist is a repeatable inspection route: confirm that the site works, remove information that no longer helps, verify important actions, and record what changes afterward. Maintenance becomes manageable when each finding receives an owner, a priority, and a review date. Four activities hold that route together: reviewing technical website health, updating outdated website content, testing important conversion elements, and monitoring ongoing performance improvements.
