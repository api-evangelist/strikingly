A shopper arrives wanting a birthday gift, a replacement part, a jacket in their size, or a look at what is new. An effective online store navigation strategy begins with those tasks and works backward to categories, collections, filters, search, and product links. This distinction matters because a tidy menu can still make shopping difficult.
