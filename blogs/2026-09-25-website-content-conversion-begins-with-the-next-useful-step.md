An article can attract the right reader, answer an important question, and still produce no meaningful next step. Website content conversion improves when it recognizes what the reader is ready to do next: compare a service, open a related guide, download a worksheet, or ask a focused question. Conversion begins with continuity, not a sudden sales pitch.
