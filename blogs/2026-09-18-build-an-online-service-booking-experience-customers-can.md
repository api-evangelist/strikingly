A booking is a record that moves from a customer's request to a reserved time, confirmation, and possible change. Online service booking succeeds when every handoff answers the next practical question. The record starts with creating simple appointment request forms, becomes credible through displaying available booking times, and becomes actionable by sending confirmation details automatically.
