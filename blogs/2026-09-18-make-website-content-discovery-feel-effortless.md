Publishing a useful page does not guarantee that anyone will find it. Visitors arrive through search results, shared links, product pages, old articles, and bookmarks, often far from the homepage. Website content discovery is the system that helps each person recognize what else is available and choose a sensible next answer without restarting from the main menu.
