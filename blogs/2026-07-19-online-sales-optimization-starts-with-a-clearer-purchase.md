Online sales optimization is the work of making each step from product interest to completed order clearer, calmer, and more persuasive. It is not only about bigger buttons or louder discounts. A store earns more sales when shoppers understand the product, trust the store, see a sensible cart, finish checkout without surprise, and have a reason to buy again.
