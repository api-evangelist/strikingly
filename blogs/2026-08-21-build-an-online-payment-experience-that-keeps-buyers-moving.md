A buyer can like the product, accept the price, and still stop at payment. Improving checkout confidence becomes difficult when the total changes, a familiar method is absent, an error gives no direction, or the page asks for trust without earning it. A strong online payment experience handles those moments as part of the purchase, not as technical details left to a gateway.
